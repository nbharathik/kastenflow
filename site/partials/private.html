Backup

Never lose a note

KastenFlow backs your notes up to a private GitHub repository that belongs to you. Set it up once, in a few clicks.

1. Sign in

Sign in with GitHub using a code, or use a token or the GitHub command line instead.

2. A private repo

KastenFlow makes a private repository for you, or you pick one you already have.

3. Backed up

Every change is backed up within minutes. The status bar says how many changes are still waiting.

Your notes and their full history are backed up. Saved chats and app settings are not, because they stay on your computer. Very large files (over 100 MB) are left out and listed. If the repository is made public, backup pauses until it is private again. Nothing is ever force-pushed.

Plugins

Switch on what you need

KastenFlow starts light. Extra tools are official plugins that you switch on in Settings. Turning one off never hides your files.

draw.io

Flowcharts, architecture drawings and sketches in an editor of their own, shown as pictures in pages, slides and whiteboards.

LaTeX

Write papers in your vault with a PDF preview that follows your typing, errors that point to the line, and Send to LaTeX from any page.

Browser

A browser tab inside KastenFlow, web previews in your slides, and saving pages as papers or captures.

Pages can also draw themselves. A smart page shows a dashboard, a map or a timeline of your notes, and runs in a closed box with no network. Start from a ready-made page, or ask your AI to build one.

Privacy

Your notes stay on your computer

KastenFlow works offline with files in a folder you choose. AI and online backups are optional.

No account

Open a folder and start writing. No KastenFlow account is needed.

Local files

Your notes are Markdown files you can open in other apps. Choose where to keep your backups.

Optional connections

Connect AI or an online backup when you need it. API keys are stored in your system keychain.

AI tools

Optional AI assistance

Connect your preferred model to ask about notes, summarise pages or work on a draft. AI changes are marked on the page and listed in History.

Try the sample chat
Connect an MCP client 
$ claude mcp add --scope user kasten -- kasten-app --mcp --vault ~/Notes

The app is the server. Settings → AI agents copies this line with its path on your computer.

Supports Anthropic's API, OpenAI-compatible servers and local models. AI is optional.

  • Preview answers.Review writing suggestions before adding them to a page.
  • Protected notes.Agents cannot permanently delete notes, rewrite history or edit locked notes.
  • Review changes.Large removals, new templates and batches of edits go to the review queue.
  • Undo a session.Revert an agent's changes from History.
  • Stored API keys.Keys stay in your system keychain and are sent only to the configured provider.
