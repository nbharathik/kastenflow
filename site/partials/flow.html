Capture

Press Ctrl N to create a note. Use the Inbox to sort notes into projects, add them to boards or assign tags.

Organise

Each project has a home with its summary, pages, to-dos and whiteboards. Drag a page onto a project to move it there.

Present

Press P on a board and each section becomes a slide, shown full screen. Escape brings back the view you had.

Find notes

Use search or the Card Library to find notes by text, kind, tag, project or date, as cards or a table.
