## How the lists work

- A new action starts from the *Next action* template: tagged `#action`, on the *Next* list.
- Something you're waiting on someone for goes on *Waiting for*, with who and since when.
- Maybe one day: *Someday*. Look at it in the weekly review.
- Projects are any outcome that takes more than one action. Each gets a KastenFlow project.
