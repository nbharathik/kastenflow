# Timeline

Dated notes on one time axis, one row for each lane, with a line for today. Click
an item to open its note; arrow keys move between items.

## Add data

Tag a note `#event` and give it the property `date` (for example `2027-03-01`).
Optional properties:

- `lane`: the row it sits in
- `end`: makes it a range from `date` to `end`
- `announced`: `false` marks the date as an estimate

To follow another tag, change `TAG` at the top of `app.js`.
