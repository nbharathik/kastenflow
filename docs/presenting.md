# Presenting

How to give a talk from KastenFlow Slides: the full-screen show, the keys, using two
displays, web pages shown over a slide, and the presenter view. Building the deck is
in [Slides](guide.md#slides).

## Start a show

| To | Press |
| --- | --- |
| Present from the first slide | **F5** |
| Present from the slide you are on | **Shift+F5** (or **Ctrl+Enter**) |
| Present with the presenter view open | **Alt+F5** (or **Ctrl+Alt+Enter**) |

Or use the **Slideshow** button at the top right: its arrow lists the other ways to
start. The keys work from anywhere in the editor, including while you are typing in
a text box. A deck embedded in a page can be presented the same way.

## Full screen and your displays

The show fills the whole screen: the title bar and taskbar go away, and the app's
sidebar, tabs, chat dock and notifications stay out of the way (a notice that
arrives during the show waits and appears afterwards). A browser tab or web preview
elsewhere in KastenFlow never shows through.

- **Leave** with **Esc**. Close an open layer first (such as the list of keys); one
  more press leaves the show. **F** switches full screen on and off without ending
  the show.
- **Two displays.** Open the **Slideshow** arrow → **Present on**. It lists
  **Automatic**, **This screen** and each display by name, and appears only when you
  have two or more. *Automatic* puts the show on the other display and the presenter
  view on the one you are working on. Your choice is remembered on this computer.
  If the display you chose is unplugged, *Automatic* is used.
- When the show ends, the window goes back to where it was, on the display it was on.
- **Arrows and progress bar** (the same menu) shows a pair of arrows and a thin bar
  on the slide. They are off by default so the slide stays clean. The pointer hides
  itself after two seconds.
- If the display changes while you present (a different scale, a cable pulled), the
  window fills the display it is on.

A slide whose shape differs from the screen's is shown whole, centred in black bars
(or the theme's colour; **Slideshow options** sets it).

## Keys during the show

| Key | Does |
| --- | --- |
| → or Space | Next step, then next slide |
| ← or Shift+Space | Back |
| ↓ and ↑ | Into and out of the backup slides |
| O | Overview of all slides |
| a number, then Enter | Go to that slide |
| B | Black screen |
| L | Laser pointer |
| F | Full screen on or off |
| ? | List of keys |
| Esc | Leave |

F5 never reloads the page during a show.

## Links and web pages

Click a web link on a slide and the page opens **over the slide** with the slide
dimmed behind it, instead of leaving your talk.

- A bar at the top shows the site and address and has **Back**, **Forward**,
  **Reload**, the three layouts (*Show over the slide*, *Show beside the slide*,
  *Full screen*), **Open in a KastenFlow tab**, **Open in my browser** and **Close**.
  Your choice of layout is remembered for the deck.
- While it loads you see a placeholder and "Loading…"; if the page cannot be shown,
  a card says so with **Try again** and **Open in my browser**. If web previews are
  off, the card has **Turn on web previews** (the Browser plugin; see
  [Plugins](plugins.md)).
- **Esc steps back one level at a time**: out of full screen, then the side or
  centre layout, then closed. It never moves the talk, and closing returns you to the
  same slide and step.
- Links that are not web pages, such as `mailto:`, open outside the show.

**Right-click a link** (in a show, in the editor, or in a page's link preview) for
the same menu everywhere: *Preview here*, *Preview beside*, *Full screen*, *Open in a
KastenFlow tab*, *Open in my browser* and *Copy link*. In the editor, the link card
also has a **Preview** button, and an embedded web page has **Preview live**.

## The presenter view

The presenter view is a second window with your notes, the next slide, a clock and
a timer. Open it with **Alt+F5**, or press **S** during the show.

- It shows what the audience sees and moves with it: press next or back in either
  window and both follow, even when you press in both at once.
- **Keys:** → Space N for next, ← P for back, B for black, a number then Enter to go to
  a slide (the typed number is shown as "Go to slide 12 ↵"), **T** to stop or start the
  timer, **R** to start it again, **Ctrl +/−/0** for the size of the notes.
- **Esc** twice in the presenter view (the second press within two seconds) ends the
  show, so a stray press does not end your talk.
- Close the window and press **S** again to open a new one: it picks up where you
  are. If the show goes quiet for five seconds (for example you reloaded the main
  window), the presenter view says "Waiting for the show…" and joins the next show by
  itself. A rehearsal keeps its view when the show ends, so a timed run is not lost.
- **Rehearse** (Slideshow → Rehearse) times each slide; see
  [Slides](guide.md#slides).

## If something goes wrong

- *The window did not go full screen.* The show still runs in the window. Press **F**
  to try again.
- *The presenter view does not open.* Nothing else is affected; the show goes on, and
  you get a notice.
- *A link says it cannot be shown.* Use **Open in my browser**.
